# flowscope

> flowscope deploys agents that map, redesign, and automate how a business actually runs, shipping working AI into production on existing systems in days, not months. It is the AI team for operating businesses that do not have one.

The blog covers services-as-software, AI delivery models, process reengineering, production reliability, and worked examples of automating back-office workflows. Every post cites named sources.
